Scope and rationale

Existing international databases record national laws, strategies and targets, and regional initiatives focus on targets and plans. We are not aware of a public source that records the implementing instruments of Asia-Pacific economies (ministerial regulations, decrees, circulars and notifications) in English together with their amendment history and legal status, across electricity, energy, manufacturing and trade. This dataset is intended to provide that record, beginning with {{ country_names }}. It supports the Power Transformation Lab's research on clean energy deployment, supply chains and trade.
